# Joshua Dolor — Period J mark

**Idea:** Geometric J with the brand period in the crook — ownable “JD.” equity.

## Colours
- J: `#3b82f6` (primary)
- Period: `#fbbf24` (secondary)
- Dark tile: `#050a14`

## Files
- `jd-symbol-blue.svg` — colour mark (web / nav)
- `jd-symbol-black.svg` / `jd-symbol-white.svg` — one-colour
- `jd-symbol-app.svg` — app icon on dark tile
- `favicon.ico`, `favicon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-*.png`

## Clear space
Keep clear space around the mark equal to the period’s diameter.

## Minimum size
- Digital: 24px height
- Favicon: use app/tile cut

## Misuse
Do not stretch, recolour arbitrarily, add drop shadows, or place the period inside the J counter.
