# Home — overrides

**Primary reference:** [Robin Noguier](https://robin-noguier.com/)  
**Secondary:** [Spencer Gabor](https://spencergabor.work/) (featured work clarity)  
**Skip:** SinanTokmak constant spin if it reads as gimmicky — only subtle scroll-linked rotation  

## Direction

- Deep black, massive outlined display name, role in small caps sans
- Huge negative space; 3D object lives in the empty right half
- Work as full-bleed scroll chapters (giant titles)
- Pill quote chip for AI positioning
- Vertical scroll progress
- No card kits, no all-caps brutalism, no neon cyberpunk

## Type

- Display: Instrument Serif (outlined)
- UI/body: DM Sans
